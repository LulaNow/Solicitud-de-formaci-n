## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Workflows not appearing?

1. Check `.windsurf/workflows/` exists
2. Restart Windsurf
3. Verify YAML frontmatter format

### Rules not being enforced?

1. Check `.windsurf/rules/` exists
2. Rules are always active (no invocation needed)
3. Restart Windsurf
